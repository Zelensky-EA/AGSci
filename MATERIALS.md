# Agriculture materials and programs

This is an editable starting taxonomy for department inventory. It draws on California Agriculture and Natural Resources pathway areas and adds practical stock families for shop, floristry, farm and classroom work. It is not an official required-materials list or a determination that any local course needs a specific purchase. Confirm actual course titles, tools, species, grades and packaging with Ag staff.

The pathway foundation covers Agricultural Business, Agricultural Mechanics, Agriscience, Animal Science, Forestry and Natural Resources, Ornamental Horticulture, and Plant and Soil Science. Additional editable program labels include Floral Design, Sustainable Agriculture, Veterinary Science, Agricultural Biology, Agricultural Chemistry, Food Science, FFA / Leadership, and SAE / Farm Projects.

## Counts and purchasing units

A named complete set, kit, box, case, bag, bale, bottle, packet or tray can be counted as one unit. Specify net contents, size and grade in the item name. If you choose to count contents individually, confirm the number of inventory units supplied by a purchase pack and use that same unit for stock, checkouts, reorder level and supplier pricing.

| Material | Suitable counted-unit example | Purchase quote conversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed, lamb grower, 50 lb bag | bag | One priced 50 lb bag supplies 1 counted bag |
| Hay, specified forage/type and bale size | bale | One priced matching bale supplies 1 counted bale |
| Nitrile gloves, medium, 100 individual gloves/box | box | One priced box supplies 1 counted box |
| Same glove product counted individually | glove | A confirmed box of 100 individual gloves supplies 100 counted gloves |
| Floral stems, named variety and grade | stem | A confirmed bunch of 10 matching stems supplies 10 counted stems |
| Drill with specified included battery/charger set | set | A matching complete set supplies 1 counted set |
| Fertilizer, exact grade/formulation and bag size | bag | One priced matching bag supplies 1 counted bag |

Stock counts are whole numbers in this edition. Bag size, pounds, gallons and length are specifications rather than automatically multiplied inventory quantities. Different species, feed formulas, grades, materials, voltages and tool models should have distinct names. No starter item has an invented price.
